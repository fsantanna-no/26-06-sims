# Goal

- ground the reps constants empirically (today: folklore)
    - cost 500, earn 1000/day, revoke 1000, max 50000, tax 10%,
      half 12h, full 24h
- output: a frontier, and the chosen point defended with data

# Method: offline ledger sweep

- `sims/ledger.lua`: fold the reps rules over an event stream
  WITHOUT the chain (pure Lua: seconds per config, not hours)
- inputs: the drivers' event streams (chat msgs, se posts+votes,
  wiki revs+revokes) -- deterministic, already extracted
- sweep grid:
    - cost   250 | 500 | 1000
    - earn   500 | 1000 | 2000
    - revoke 500 | 1000 | 2000
    - max    10k | 50k | none
    - tax    0 | 10 | 25
- on-chain confirmation: 2-3 chosen configs, small slices

# Metrics per config

- beg/resurrection rate, split innocent vs vandal
    - innocent = the friction cost of tight cost/earn
- community vs dictator revoke share (wiki gated)
    - evaluates `revoke`: can real reverters afford it?
- cap-hit rate + balance distribution over time
    - if nobody nears max: dead weight
    - if veterans pin at max: anti-whale bound working
    - capacity removed from veterans by the cap
- vandal survival time (events until death)

# What "good" means

- minimize innocent resurrections
- subject to: vandals still die; no unbounded vote power
- paper picks a frontier point and SHOWS it

# Hypotheses to test

- max 50000 only binds for multi-year veterans: it is a
  Sybil/whale bound, not a daily-economy constant
- chat members hover near 0: cap never binds for normal users
- revoke 1000 exceeds most reverters' balance at revert time
  (dictator ends up doing most moderation)
